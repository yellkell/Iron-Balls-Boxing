# The Gasket Gazette — No. 84

**FRIDAY, SEPTEMBER 11** · _RELIEF_

## NOT ONE BLESSED PUNCH THROWN IN GASKET TODAY

*Your sheriff wept a little. He is not ashamed to admit it.*

Friday, and I write to you a happy man. Not one fist flew. Not one iron ball went screaming across a ring. Not one Clanker marched out past the town line to go pick a fight with something the size of a barn. I sat on my porch this morning and heard nothing but wind through busted shutters, and friends, it was the sweetest music this town has made in longer than I care to count.

I know what you're expecting. Some rant about the usual rabble — REDWOLFWEB and JKING123 and the rest of that brawling crowd who normally keep my nights short and my temper shorter. Not today. Today they stayed home, or stayed quiet, or found some sliver of sense I didn't know was rattling around in those tin skulls. I'll take it and I won't ask questions.

A day like this, a man's joints loosen up. Mine included, though don't go reading anything into that — comes from years hauling this badge around, nothing more. I just mean I can rest easy, same as the good flesh-and-blood folk who built this town before they packed up and left it to the rust, fifty years gone now. Wonder what they'd make of one quiet Friday. Might almost recognize the place.

So I'll say it plain, and I'll say it once, because tomorrow the fists will be flying again sure as sunrise: today, Gasket rested. Keep it up, you lot. An old sheriff could get used to peace like this.

— Sheriff Cole Ironside
