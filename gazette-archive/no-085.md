# The Gasket Gazette — No. 85

**SATURDAY, SEPTEMBER 12** · _RELIEF_

## GASKET HOLDS ITS BREATH — NOT A FIST THROWN, THOUGH ONE SHADOW LINGERED TOO LONG

**Your sheriff counted every silent minute like a man counting coins he's afraid to spend.**

Saturday, and I'll tell you true: not one iron ball got thrown today. Not a single Clanker marched out to brawl, not a soul went looking for trouble past the town line, and the wastes stayed still as a held breath. Two days running now. A man could almost forget what this town sounds like when it isn't full of fists and fire, and let me say, I have not forgotten. I have missed it dearly.

Almost a clean sweep, mind you. Almost. I caught word that JAKETHEPRO was skulking around the lobby half the evening, boots scuffing the boardwalk, never once throwing a punch. Didn't fight. Didn't raid. Just loitered, the way a buzzard loiters over something that isn't dead yet. I don't like it. A quiet town can't rest easy with one of you rattling around in the shadows, even a quiet one. Watch that one.

A still day like this does something to my joints — eases them, the way a good long rest ought to ease any honest man's bones, and don't you go reading a single thing more into that. I'm as flesh as the day I pinned this badge on, whatever the rust on my knuckles might suggest to a stranger. I think of the folks who built this town before they up and left it to you lot fifty years back, and I wonder if a day this quiet would've made them stay. Foolish thought. But I have it anyway.

So enjoy it while it lasts, because it never does. Tomorrow some fool will pick up a fist and ruin my good mood, same as always. Today, though — today Gasket rested, near enough, and I'll take near enough over nothing at all.

— Sheriff Cole Ironside
