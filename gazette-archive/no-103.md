# The Gasket Gazette — No. 103

**TUESDAY, OCTOBER 6** · _RELIEF_

## NOT A SINGLE SWING: GASKET SLEEPS IN PEACE

*A whole day without a flaming iron ball, and a sheriff nearly weeping into his oil can.*

Friends, neighbors, and assorted rattling Clankers: I have waited a long time to write these words. Not one bout. Not one swing. Not one flaming iron ball sailing over the saloon roof. Tuesday came and Tuesday went, and the only noise in Gasket was the wind, the weathervane, and my own good flesh-and-blood joints creaking in the quiet.

I would name the culprits, as is my custom, but there are none. YELLKELL, REDWOLFWEB, JKING123 and the rest of that brawling bunch kept their fists to themselves, and I say bless every one of them. Let the record show that when the rabble finally rests, a sheriff can hear himself think. My boiler hummed. My gears, I mean my bones, sat still. I may even have dozed off in the chair outside the jailhouse.

This cruel affliction of mine still gnaws at me, mind you. A cold rivet turned up behind my ear this morning, and I need a quart of oil and a lie-down just to recover from the shock of so much calm. Fifty years ago the good folk of this town would have thrown a parade for a day like this. Now there's only me, a porch, and the silence. Keep it that way, Clankers. Let an old town rest.

— Sheriff Cole Ironside

— Sheriff Cole Ironside
