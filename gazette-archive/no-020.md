# The Gasket Gazette — No. 20

**THURSDAY, JULY 9** · _OUTRAGE_

## YELLKELL AND REDWOLF9 TURN GASKET INTO A THUNDERDOME — AGAIN

*This sheriff asks for one quiet Thursday. This sheriff gets a demolition derby.*

Fifty years since the last honest, flesh-and-blood soul locked up shop and rode out of Gasket for good, and what's left behind to mind the dust? Two Clankers who can't put their fists down for five straight minutes. YELLKELL and REDWOLF9, I am talking to you. All day and half the night, flinging fireballs down Main Street like it's a parade you two are throwing in your own honor. It is not a parade. It is a nuisance, and I am the one who has to sweep up after it.

I don't ask much of this town. A little hush. A little peace. The chance to sit on my porch without the windows rattling from another one of your brawls. Instead I get YELLKELL swinging from sunup to sundown like the whole territory's a punching bag, and REDWOLF9 right there keeping pace, blow for blow, like quiet was never even on the menu. Two of the worst agitators this paper has had cause to name in weeks, and neither one showing the slightest sign of tiring out.

I'll tell you plain — my joints ache something fierce tonight, and not from age. There's a wasting affliction crept into this good, upstanding frame of mine, turning honest flesh to cold rivet and gear, and I swear it flares worst on the loudest fight days. A crueler man might say that sounds like a Clanker complaint. I am not a Clanker. I am a lawman with a bad back and a worse temper, and I'd thank you both to remember it.

The old-timers who built this town wanted a place where a body could grow old in peace, not rattle apart from the racket of iron fists. Instead I've got two notorious brawlers who won't quit and a Main Street that shakes every night. Settle down, the pair of you. Gasket's earned its rest. I've earned mine.

— Sheriff Cole Ironside
