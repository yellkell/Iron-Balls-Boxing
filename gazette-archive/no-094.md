# The Gasket Gazette — No. 94

**SUNDAY, SEPTEMBER 27** · _DESPAIR_

## ONE LONE FOOL KEEPS THE RACKET ALIVE

*Four fresh faces roll into a town too tired to greet them, as IRON-26E6 spoils an otherwise blessed hush.*

Gasket came close today. Closer than it's come in weeks. The dust sat still, the shutters stayed shut, and for one shining stretch of the afternoon this old sheriff let himself believe -- believe! -- that his Clankers had finally run out of fists to throw. Then, same as always, one bolt-brained brawler had to go and ruin it.

IRON-26E6. Write it down, folks, so you know who to glare at next time you see rust round these parts. One tussle. That's all it took to crack the silence I'd been polishing since sunup. One rattle of iron fists and the peace I'd been guarding with my own two creaking hands went right out the window, same as it always does. I'd ask what could possibly be worth it, but I already know the answer: nothing. Never is.

And as if that weren't enough disturbance for one Sunday, four new units came rolling into town on the evening dust -- IRON-F458, IRON-71A5, VFFFF, and IRON-C3CC, fresh boots and shinier rivets, none of them wise yet to how a decent town is meant to run. I watched them from the porch with my joints aching something fierce, the way they always do when the wind turns, and thought -- not for the first time -- of the flesh-and-blood folk who used to fill these streets before the last of them packed up and left this place to the metal fifty years gone. They'd have known how to welcome a stranger without throwing a punch first. These four will learn Gasket's ways soon enough, one way or the other.

Still -- one brawler, no raiding parties out past the town line, no war party marching home reeking of victory and worse. Small mercies. I'll take them where I can get them, sit back down in this chair that creaks louder than I do, and hope tomorrow's paper has even less to report. A sheriff can dream.

— Sheriff Cole Ironside
