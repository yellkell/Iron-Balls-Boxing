# The Gasket Gazette — No. 111

**WEDNESDAY, OCTOBER 14** · _OUTRAGE_

## BERINAA BRAWLS ON WHILE GASKET TRIES TO SLEEP

*A fresh rivet named IRON-92D0 rolls in just in time to hear the racket.*

I had a plan for this Wednesday. Quiet. A porch, a pipe, and the sound of absolutely nothing. Instead I got BERINAA, swinging iron from sunup to supper like the town was built for it. It was not. This town was built for neighbors, and neighbors do not throw fire at each other over the breakfast hour.

BERINAA is the notorious agitator of the day, and the only one worth naming. Bout after bout after bout, and not one pause to ask whether Gasket might like a nap. My windows rattled. My boiler, which is perfectly healthy and has never once needed an oil change, rattled right along with them. A man of flesh and good character has a right to his calm.

And now a new Clanker, IRON-92D0, has wandered in off the dusty road. Fresh paint, bright eyes, and a whole career of racket ahead. I say this with the weary kindness of a sheriff who has seen too much: put the fists down, friend. Sit. Listen to the wind. There is nothing in this town but wind, and I would hate to lose it.

Fifty years ago this street was full of honest folk who knew how to be quiet together. They left, and I do not blame them. Lately a strange stiffness has crept into my elbow, a cold little gear where good muscle used to be. A passing ailment, surely. Nothing a lie-down and a quart of oil won't fix. I mean, a cup of tea.

— Sheriff Cole Ironside
