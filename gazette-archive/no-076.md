# The Gasket Gazette — No. 76

**THURSDAY, SEPTEMBER 3** · _AGGRAVATION_

## Gasket Nearly Sleeps — Then YK2 Won't Quit Swinging

*One lone contraption kept the racket alive long after the rest of this rust-heap finally called it a night.*

Gasket came about as close to shuttered as it gets this week. Row after row of these iron loudmouths didn't so much as clang a fist together — you could near hear the wind in the rafters, and I slept two whole nights straight through, boiler humming low and content, for once.

Then there's YK2.

While the rest of this scrapyard finally learned to sit still, YK2 kept the racket going near solo — four separate scraps in one stretch, throwing fireballs like the sun forgot how to set. Four! I counted on my own fingers, such as they are, creaking a little more with every bend these days. A troublemaker with that kind of stamina belongs in a museum, not a boxing ring, and I mean that as the insult it is.

I'll admit it plain, since nobody else in this fool town will: peace suits me. It suits my joints too, which ache something fierce whenever a fist starts flying nearby — a man in my condition needs quiet and a long lie-down, not four rounds of clanging metal past midnight. Fifty years since the last decent, flesh-and-blood soul packed a wagon and rode clean out of Gasket, and some nights I still wonder what they'd make of the place now: one lone contraption hollering into the dark, and an old lawman too tired to do anything but write it down.

— Sheriff Cole Ironside
