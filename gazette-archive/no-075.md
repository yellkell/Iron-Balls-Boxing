# The Gasket Gazette — No. 75

**WEDNESDAY, SEPTEMBER 2** · _RELIEF_

## GASKET NEARLY GOES QUIET — ONE RATTLER WON'T LET IT LAST

*Sheriff Ironside counts the stillest stretch this town has seen in weeks, spoiled only by a single stubborn clatter from IRON-4C25.*

For most of this stretch, dear reader, your Sheriff near wept with joy. No mobs in the square. No war parties marching for the wastes. Just the wind through the broken shutters and the good, honest quiet Gasket hasn't tasted in a coon's age. I let myself believe, for one shining moment, that the Clankers had finally worn themselves out.

Then came the clatter. IRON-4C25, banging away out there not once but twice, apparently unable to sit still while the rest of this town caught its breath. Twice! In a hush this deep, that racket carries clear to the county line. I don't know what fool notion possesses a unit to go looking for a fight when there's nobody left to complain about the noise but an old lawman and his aching joints — but there it is, plain as the rattle in my own knees this morning.

And don't think I didn't notice how that last sentence sounded. A man of flesh don't rattle. I'll have you know it's this blasted affliction creeping through me again — some cruel wasting sickness turning good honest bone to gearwork, nothing more, and I'll thank you not to speculate otherwise. The Gasket I remember, back when it was full of real folk instead of iron and rust, never once made me explain myself like this.

Still — one rattler does not a riot make, and for that small mercy I am grateful. Lay down your fists, the rest of you, and let an old town keep its hush a little longer.

— Sheriff Cole Ironside
