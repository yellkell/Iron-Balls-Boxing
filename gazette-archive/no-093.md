# The Gasket Gazette — No. 93

**SATURDAY, SEPTEMBER 26** · _WARY_

## SILENCE FALLS ON GASKET — THEN A STRANGER ROLLS IN

*Not a single iron ball thrown all day — but the peace didn't last past the depot gate.*

For one glorious stretch, Gasket held still. No fists flew. No fireballs scorched the boardwalk. Sheriff Cole Ironside stood on his porch this morning and heard nothing but wind through the broken shutters — the sweetest song this town has known in longer than he cares to count.

Then the depot bell rang, and a fresh set of gears came rattling up Main Street under the name ETHAN. Cole watched it roll past the saloon with the wide-eyed look every newcomer wears before it learns where the fists start flying, and felt his hard-won quiet curdle a little at the edges.

He'll tell you he's watching ETHAN the way any decent flesh-and-blood lawman watches a stranger — never mind that his own knees have been creaking louder than the saloon doors lately, or that he traded a sling for a fresh coat of oil just last week. A trick of the joints, he insists, nothing more. The kind of trouble that never once bothered the honest folk who packed up and left this town fifty years back.

So mark it down, rare as it is: a whole day Gasket's iron didn't clash. Let it stand as fair warning to the new arrival — there's a hush worth keeping here, ETHAN, and Cole Ironside will personally walk out any Clanker fool enough to break it.

— Sheriff Cole Ironside
