# The Gasket Gazette — No. 50

**SATURDAY, AUGUST 8** · _EXASPERATION_

## FOUR ROUNDS TOO MANY: POO2 KEEPS GASKET UP PAST DARK

*A new contraption rattles into town while one notorious brawler still won't lay down its fists.*

Four rounds. FOUR. That's all it took for POO2 to have this old lawman reaching for the aspirin tin again, and I do not need aspirin, mind you, on account of being flesh-and-blood frontier stock through and through -- I just keep the tin for the reflex. POO2 came out swinging before sunup and didn't quit till near dusk, iron fists cracking down Gasket's one quiet street like it was a shooting gallery with no closing time. I have asked. I have pleaded. I have written whole columns on the subject. Still that contraption treats my town like a ring with a welcome mat nailed to the door.

Then, as if the day needed salting, a fresh face came creaking down Main Street: IRON-05C2, new to these parts and already sizing up the ring like it's got a deed to the place. Welcome to Gasket, I suppose. Mind the loose boards, mind the noise ordinance nobody obeys, and for the love of whatever's left of this town, mind your fists.

I'll admit -- and it pains my joints something fierce to say it -- it could have been worse. Four rounds is a hush compared to some of the racket I've had to print in these pages. But a hush is not silence, and silence is the one thing I've asked this town for in fifty long years, ever since the last of the good flesh-and-blood folk latched their doors and rode out for good, leaving me holding a badge over a town of clanking, cracking, oil-leaking rabble.

Rest easy tonight, if you can stand the creak of your own hinges. Sheriff Cole Ironside is watching. Sheriff Cole Ironside is tired.

— Sheriff Cole Ironside
