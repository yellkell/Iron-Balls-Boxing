# The Gasket Gazette — No. 37

**SUNDAY, JULY 26** · _IRRITATION_

## TWO CLANKERS COULDN'T LET A SUNDAY LIE

*A quiet town almost made it through the day whole, until YELLKELL and MAXMANGO went and ruined it.*

Sunday, and for most of it Gasket held its breath in something close to peace. I had my boots up, my joints at rest, and not one lick of trouble on the wind. Then, same as always, a couple of iron-fisted troublemakers decided the hush was an insult they couldn't abide.

YELLKELL and MAXMANGO, I'm looking square at the both of you. One tussle apiece is all it took to crack the calm clean in two, fists and flame going where a decent quiet oughta sit undisturbed. You'd think two lonesome scuffles wouldn't rattle a lawman, but it's the principle of the thing: this town does not owe you a stage.

My knees ache something fierce just typing this up, and don't think I haven't noticed the rust creeping up my own forearm again -- a man of flesh and grit doesn't creak like a rusty gate hinge, and yet here I am, creaking. Some cruel affliction, I tell you, turning good honest bone to cold iron a little more each year. Wasn't always like this. Wasn't always Clankers as far as the eye could see, either.

Fifty years the real folk of Gasket have been gone now, and still this town can't manage one full day without a couple of tin-knuckled hotheads finding a reason to swing. YELLKELL, MAXMANGO -- go find a porch, go find a rocking chair, go find anything quieter than each other's company. Gasket's earned its rest. I've earned mine.

— Sheriff Cole Ironside
