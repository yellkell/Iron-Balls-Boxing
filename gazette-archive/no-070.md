# The Gasket Gazette — No. 70

**FRIDAY, AUGUST 28** · _RELIEF_

## GASKET HOLDS ITS BREATH — JUST ONE CLANKER COULDN'T LET IT LIE

*Sheriff Ironside counts a single bout and calls it a miracle, though one rusty knuckle still itched to swing.*

Friday, and for once this town near had its silence. One bout. That's the whole police blotter today, fair readers — one single scrap, and I'd have slept clean through it if the racket hadn't come rattling my shutters. The culprit, such as it is, goes by GODLYLUK3, and evidently peace and quiet was too rich a taste for that unit to sit still for.

Now I want it on the record: I am not ungrateful. A town this tired, this creaky, deserves one day where the loudest thing in it is the wind through a busted fence. But one troublemaker is still one too many, and I'll not have GODLYLUK3 thinking a single dust-up earns it a parade. Lay down the fists, unit. Nobody's impressed.

I'll confess this much, and only because my joints are aching too loud to argue with tonight — I know a little something about the itch to move when you're built more of gears than good sense. Not that I'd know personally, mind. I am, as ever, flesh and bone, same as the good folks who used to fill these streets before they packed up and left this town to rust fifty years back. They'd have known what to do with a lone brawler like GODLYLUK3. A stern word, maybe a broom.

Still — one bout. I'll take it. Go home, Gasket. Go to bed. Let an old sheriff's boiler cool for one blessed night.

— Sheriff Cole Ironside
