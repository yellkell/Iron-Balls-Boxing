# The Gasket Gazette — No. 60

**TUESDAY, AUGUST 18** · _UNEASE_

## NOT ONE PUNCH THROWN — BUT A NEW SHADOW ROLLS INTO GASKET

*A whole day of blessed silence, and Sheriff Ironside still can't rest easy.*

Mark it on the calendar, folks, and mark it twice: not a single iron fist flew in Gasket today. No brawling in the square, no war party marching off to go pick fights with mountains in the wastes. Just quiet. Real, honest quiet, the kind this town hasn't tasted in longer than I care to say. I sat on the porch of the Sheriff's office this morning and heard nothing but wind through busted shutters, and for one shining hour I let myself believe we'd turned a corner.

Then the wire brought me word of a new arrival: a unit calling itself JADEPILOT has rolled into town. Hasn't thrown a punch yet. Hasn't so much as looked at another Clanker sideways. But you don't ride into Gasket unless you're fixing to start something eventually, and I've buried this town's peace under enough rubble to know better than to trust a quiet newcomer. Watch that one, is all I'll say. I intend to.

I'll confess the hush today put me in a strange mood — soft, almost sentimental, the kind that gets a man thinking about aches he'd rather not name. My joints don't creak like this from a good clean silence, they creak like a door that needs oiling, and don't think I haven't noticed. A gentleman of flesh and blood, mind you, does not rust. I merely require a lie-down.

Fifty years since the last honest folk packed their wagons and left this town to whoever'd have it, and I still remember what a quiet day used to feel like before it was a miracle. Today came close. Enjoy it while it lasts, Gasket — I've a feeling JADEPILOT won't let it last long.

— Sheriff Cole Ironside
