# The Gasket Gazette — No. 48

**THURSDAY, AUGUST 6** · _RESIGNATION_

## ONE STUBBORN FIST WAS ALL IT TOOK TO RUIN A PERFECT THURSDAY

*Sheriff Cole Ironside comes within one knuckle of the silence he's begged this town for since the flesh-and-blood folks packed up and left.*

I want it on the record: Thursday came about as close to quiet as Gasket gets anymore. A whole day, near enough silent, and for one shining hour I let myself believe this town had finally listened to reason. Then, same as always, along came YK, throwing punches like the rest of us don't have somewhere quieter to be. One brawler. That's all it takes to spoil a good peace these days, and wouldn't you know it's the same one every single time. I'm starting to think YK and I are the only two souls left in this town who never sleep, and I don't care for the company.

Three new contraptions rolled in through the gate besides, quiet as you please, setting up shop without so much as a howdy to the fella who's kept this street standing longer than any of you have had hinges. CRYSTALZACH's been skulking around a spell already, but IRON-468F and IRON-E7C8 are fresh serial numbers, and I expect I'll be writing about their nonsense soon enough. Welcome to Gasket. Try not to swing anything.

My back's been acting up somethin' fierce tonight — a stiffness in the shoulder that no honest doctor's ever put a name to, and I won't hear a word about oil, thank you kindly, on account of I am a man, not a machine. Just old bones creaking under an old man's grudge, same as any frontier lawman carries after fifty years of watching a good town go quiet, then go to rust.

So here's my report, one lonely line of it: a single set of knuckles kept the peace from holding. Next time, YK, let it hold. Give an old man's joints — and his patience — a rest.

— Sheriff Cole Ironside
