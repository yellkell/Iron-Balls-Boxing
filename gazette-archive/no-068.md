# The Gasket Gazette — No. 68

**WEDNESDAY, AUGUST 26** · _RELIEF_

## NOT ONE IRON BALL THROWN — BUT A STRANGER RIDES INTO GASKET

*A whole day without a single fist raised in this town, and Sheriff Cole Ironside is almost too scared to jinx it by saying so.*

Reader, mark this date. Not one bout. Not one flaming ball flung across a duel yard. Not one titan dragged down screaming in the wastes. For twenty-four blessed hours, Gasket Territory held its breath and, for once, its fists. This old lawman sat on his porch, oiled his knees against the evening chill, and heard nothing but wind through busted shutters. A body could almost mistake it for the town it used to be.

Don't get comfortable. A new callsign posted itself at the edge of town today — IRON-F2C2, rolled in from parts unknown, boots not even dusty yet. Didn't throw a punch, didn't pick a fight, just... arrived. That is exactly how they all start. I've watched enough of these Clankers creak into Gasket wide-eyed and quiet before they're hurling fire at their neighbors inside a week. I'll be keeping an eye on this one.

Fifty years since the last honest, flesh-and-blood family boarded up their windows and rode out of here for good, and still I hold out hope some quiet folk might yet wander back to a town that, for one single day, behaved itself. I mean that sincerely, and if my voice cracked a little writing it, blame the dust in these old lungs — not, whatever the town gossips whisper, a hinge that wants greasing.

So enjoy it while it lasts, Gasket. One newcomer standing still is the closest thing to peace this territory's had in ages. Tomorrow they'll likely be swinging like the rest. Today, at least, nobody bled iron.

— Sheriff Cole Ironside
