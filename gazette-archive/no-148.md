# The Gasket Gazette — No. 148

**FRIDAY, NOVEMBER 20** · _BLISS_

## Eleven Days of Peace: Sheriff Holds a Town Meeting, Nobody Shows

*An eleventh straight day without a bout or a war party, and the law calls the first public gathering in fifty years. Attendance: one.*

Eleven days. Eleven! That is one more than ten, and ten was already the best stretch this town has seen since I pinned on this star. Since our last edition not one Clanker has thrown a punch in Gasket. Not one flaming iron ball has gone sailing through the saloon window. Not one posse has marched out past the town line to pester the wild engines in the wastes. It is a Friday, and Gasket is so quiet I can hear the dust settle.

A peace this long deserves to be made official, so I rang the old church bell and called a town meeting, the first since the Hargroves, the Pruitts and every other honest flesh-and-blood family rolled out of here fifty years ago. I set out twelve chairs. I lit the lamps. I wrote a speech about harmony and good neighbours and keeping your fists in your pockets. And I sat there and gave it to an empty room, which listened better than any crowd of Clankers ever has. I moved that the peace continue. The motion carried, one vote to none.

The garden report, as promised: the rust bloom on the washers behind the general store has spread to all three rows, and I believe one of them has grown a bolt. I am taking that as a good sign. I am less pleased with my own health. The wasting sickness got into my left elbow this week, and now it only bends if I tap it with a wrench first. A doctor would surely call it rheumatism. I call it an outrage against a decent man's body, and I treated it with a nap and a warm quart of oil.

So here is my word to every dented rattletrap in Gasket Territory, from YELLKELL and REDWOLFWEB to JKING123 and CRYSTALZACH and the rest of you clanking sorts: eleven days, and not one fist raised. You missed a fine meeting. Next time, come sit in a chair. Quietly. And leave your iron balls at the door.

— Sheriff Cole Ironside
