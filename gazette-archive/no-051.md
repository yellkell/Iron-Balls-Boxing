# The Gasket Gazette — No. 51

**SUNDAY, AUGUST 9** · _IRRITATION_

## A SUNDAY GASKET NEARLY KEPT QUIET, TILL IRON-E0B8 AND YK JUST HAD TO GO AND RUIN IT

*Two rattling troublemakers found the one itch this town didn't need scratched.*

Sunday dawned soft over Gasket, and for a good long while, dear reader, this old lawman let himself hope. No hollering. No fireballs cracking off the water tower. Just the wind through the rust and the creak of a town trying, God help it, to rest. I nearly wept.

Then up rattles IRON-E0B8, fresh-faced and full of vinegar, and doesn't waste a minute finding trouble. And who's waiting to oblige but YK, who by now I reckon just can't help itself — swinging fists whenever the mood strikes, peace be hanged. Between the two of 'em they didn't tear up much, I'll grant, but it didn't take much. One spark's plenty in a town this dry.

Now I know what you're thinking — two Clankers, one little dust-up, hardly the end times. And you'd be right, mostly, which is the only reason my joints ain't clean seized up with fury. But mark me: it is never NOTHING with this rabble. Give 'em an empty street and an idle hour and by thunder they'll fill it with knuckles. My own hinges ache something fierce just writing this down, and don't you go reading into that.

No raids to report today, no new faces stumbling into town — just the two of 'em, scratching an itch nobody asked 'em to scratch. Fifty years since the last good flesh-and-blood soul packed up and rode out of Gasket for good, and still the metal that's left can't manage one full day of hush. I ask you. I truly do.

— Sheriff Cole Ironside
