# The Gasket Gazette — No. 62

**THURSDAY, AUGUST 20** · _EXASPERATION_

## SO CLOSE: ONE CLANKER RUINS A PERFECT HUSH

*Gasket held its breath for a whole, blessed silence — and JADEPILOT just had to go and spoil it.*

For one shining stretch of this Thursday morning, dear reader, Gasket was quiet. Truly quiet. The kind of quiet this old town hasn't earned in fifty years, not since the last of the good flesh-and-blood folk locked their doors and walked on down the road for good. I stood on the porch of this very newspaper office and let myself believe, just for a minute, that the iron mob had finally worn itself out.

Then came the clatter. One single scuffle, cracking the hush clean in two, and I didn't even need to check the wire to know whose racket it was. JADEPILOT, you had the whole quiet town at your mercy and you couldn't leave it be for one lousy morning. One brawl. That's all it takes for this whole fragile peace to come apart at the seams, and you're the one who tore it.

I'll admit my joints locked up something fierce at the sound — tensed clean through, like always happens when the noise starts, this cruel affliction of mine turning me to gears and cold rivets a little more each time some Clanker decides the peace is optional. A flesh-and-blood man shouldn't creak like that. But here we are.

So close, Gasket. So close to a headline I'd have loved to print: nothing happened. Maybe tomorrow. Lay down the fists, the lot of you, and let an old, worn-out town rest.

— Sheriff Cole Ironside
