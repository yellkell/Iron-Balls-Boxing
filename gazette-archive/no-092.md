# The Gasket Gazette — No. 92

**FRIDAY, SEPTEMBER 25** · _DISQUIET_

## NOT ONE FIST FLEW TODAY, BUT FIVE STRANGERS JUST ROLLED INTO GASKET

*A blessed hush falls over the territory — and Sheriff Ironside can't decide whether to celebrate or bar the door.*

For one whole day, dear readers, this editor's desk sat quiet. No fireballs. No hollering. No iron fists cracking against iron jaws out past my window. I sat in my chair, closed my eyes, and for a moment — just a moment — Gasket sounded like a town again. A man could almost hear the wind.

But peace in this territory never lasts longer than it takes to write about it. Word from the gate says five new faces rolled in on the evening dust: CHEAPAHH, TRAUMA, IRON-5C44, IRON-D52B, and IRON-EAFA. Haven't thrown a punch yet, not one of them. But I know that look. I've swept enough scorch marks off Main Street to know a wagonload of trouble when it parks itself at the saloon.

My own joints ache something fierce just watching them unload — a lie-down and a quart of oil wouldn't go amiss tonight, if a decent flesh-and-blood man could still find either in this rusted-out excuse for a town. Fifty years since the last honest folk packed up and rode east, and all that's left to greet the newcomers is me and my aching bones.

So enjoy the hush while it holds, Gasket. To CHEAPAHH, TRAUMA, and the three IRONs fresh off the trail: unpack your bags, sure. But if any one of you so much as looks at another Clanker sideways, know that this old lawman is watching, and he is tired, and he would very much like his quiet back.

— Sheriff Cole Ironside
