# The Gasket Gazette — No. 41

**THURSDAY, JULY 30** · _IRRITATION_

## ONE TIN GOD KEEPS A GHOST TOWN UP ALL NIGHT

*Sheriff Ironside finds Gasket nearly empty and still can't get a moment's peace.*

Two souls. That's all that stirred in Gasket Territory today. Two, in a town that once had streets full of good, honest, breathing folk. And of those two, one just had to go and ruin it for everybody by picking a fight not once, not twice, but three separate times before the sun even set.

WYATTDAG0D. That's the name it's calling itself now, like tossing flaming iron balls at your neighbor three times in an afternoon makes you a deity instead of a nuisance. I've met gods. Read about 'em, anyhow. Not one of 'em spent their holy hours clanging around a near-empty street looking for something to punch. You want a title that fits, try troublemaker. Try the last loud thing rattling in a town that's mostly gone quiet.

And quiet is exactly what unnerves me about days like this. When there's only two of you left out here and one's still swinging, it tells me just how thin this town has worn. Fifty years since the flesh-and-blood folks loaded their wagons and rode off rather than watch this happen, and here we are, down to a couple of rattling units, and even that's one too rowdy for its own good.

My knees were aching something fierce watching the reports roll in today. Old injury. None of your business what kind. Point is, a man of my constitution shouldn't have to grit through three separate disturbances from one lone brawler while the rest of this territory can't even be bothered to show up.

So hear me, WYATTDAG0D: there's hardly anybody left out here to fight. Have some respect for the quiet the rest of 'em managed. This old town's earned its rest, even if you haven't learned yours.

— Sheriff Cole Ironside
