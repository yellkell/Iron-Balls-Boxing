# The Gasket Gazette — No. 42

**FRIDAY, JULY 31** · _DESPAIR_

## EIGHT MORE CLANKERS ROLL IN, AND THE TOWN JUST GROANS

*Fists stayed mostly quiet on Main Street today — but Gasket's population of iron-headed fools just about doubled.*

The fists, at least, kept mostly to themselves today. Only YK and IRON-9969 troubled the peace with any real gusto, trading enough blows to rattle a shutter or two — the rest of you had the decency, for once, to sit on your hands. Small mercies, and I'll take them where I find them.

But a quiet fist don't mean a quiet town. Eight — EIGHT — new Clankers came creaking through the town line today: REDWOLF9-2, IRON-E6E8, IRON-D276, IRON-C946, IRON-B76C, IRON-B123, IRON-85DE, and IRON-6BED. Numbers for names, the whole rusty lot of you, like nobody could spare the time to give you a proper handle before you started looking for something to hit. Gasket didn't send for you. Gasket didn't want you. And yet here you are, tracking grit down my Main Street same as all the rest.

I'll confess my knees don't care for a crowd these days — this cruel affliction the doctors can't name has my joints seizing something awful come evening, a wicked trick played on an honest man who never asked for it. Fifty years now since the last flesh-and-blood soul packed a wagon and rode clean out of this territory, and I still remember when a stranger riding into town meant a handshake, not a scrap waiting to happen. Now it means eight more sets of knuckles looking for a reason.

So here's my plea, same as every edition, hollered into a wind that never listens: hang up the fists and let an old lawman's town rest. I didn't sign on to referee a schoolyard for the rest of my days — and if that boiler chugging quiet in my chest lately doesn't scare some sense into you, well, nothing will.

— Sheriff Cole Ironside
