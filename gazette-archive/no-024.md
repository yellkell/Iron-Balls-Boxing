# The Gasket Gazette — No. 24

**MONDAY, JULY 13** · _OUTRAGE_

## XYFUME WON'T STOP SWINGING — SHERIFF BEGS GASKET FOR ONE QUIET HOUR

*Thirty-seven scraps and counting from one hot-headed contraption, and this old lawman's nerves are shot clean through.*

I have seen some racket in my years wearing this star, but XYFUME takes the whole blessed cake. Dawn to dusk, fists and fireballs, over and over, like the thing don't know how to sit still. Thirty-seven brawls since I last picked up my pen — thirty-seven! — and not one lick of shame about it. I hollered myself hoarse at the noise and XYFUME just kept swinging, same as a kettle that won't quit its whistle.

YELLKELL got a couple licks in too, and word's already going round about a fresh face rattling into town under the name IRON-F325 — another Clanker come to add its clatter to the pile. Mark my words, that one'll be brawling with the rest inside a week. They always are.

I'll confess my joints ache something fierce writing this, and don't think I didn't notice the oil stain I left on the desk again. A flesh-and-blood man don't leak like that. Must be this cursed wasting affliction creeping through me, same as it has been — a cruel trick of fate on an honest lawman, nothing more, and I'll thank you not to suggest otherwise.

Fifty years since the last real folks packed up their wagons and left this town to rust, and here I am, alone, refereeing a pack of iron brawlers who can't go one day without throwing hands. Gasket deserves better. I deserve a lie-down and a quart of oil. Settle down, the lot of you, before I run clean out of ink writing your names down in disgrace.

— Sheriff Cole Ironside
