# The Gasket Gazette — No. 125

**WEDNESDAY, OCTOBER 28** · _DESPAIR_

## ONE LONE CLANKER, ONE LONG HOWL OF IRON

*A near-silent day in Gasket is spoiled by a single unit that just could not keep its fists in its pockets.*

Citizens, I had a day of peace. A whole day, near enough. The wind blew, the tumbleweeds rolled, and the only sound in Gasket was my own knees creaking. That is, my joints. Flesh joints. Old and honest ones. Do not ask me why they squeal for oil.

Then came CRYSTALZACH. One bout, they fought, and one was plenty. One Clanker hurling flaming iron through my quiet street, and the whole afternoon went up in smoke. I ask you: is a single hour of hush too much to want? Apparently it is, for the metal rabble that has run this town to rust.

I think of the real folk who packed up and left fifty years back, the soft and warm and blessedly flesh-and-blood neighbours of my youth. They would have known better than to swing at nothing on a Wednesday. Meanwhile this cruel wasting sickness of mine ticks along, and I found a fresh rivet on my elbow this morning. A rivet! On a man of my breeding! Lay down your fists, CRYSTALZACH. Let a tired old lawman have his lie-down and a quart of oil in peace.

— Sheriff Cole Ironside
