# The Gasket Gazette — No. 23

**SUNDAY, JULY 12** · _OUTRAGE_

## IRON FISTS RATTLE GASKET TILL SUNDOWN

*Sheriff Ironside counts the wreckage and wonders what a quiet Sunday even sounds like.*

Thirty-nine scuffles, by my count, and not one apology among them. MAXMANGO led the racket again, swinging fists from sunup to sundown like the whole town was its own personal punching bag, and climbing a notch on account of it. That is not something to be proud of, Clanker. That is a confession.

REDWOLF9 was right there stoking the fire, and YELLKELL, who ought to know better sitting pretty at the top of this heap, couldn't resist joining the fray either. Three units, thirty-nine fights, and my boiler aching worse than it has all week from the noise of it. My joints don't take kindly to a day like this one, and neither does this town.

Gasket wasn't built for brawling. Fifty years back there were real folks here, flesh and blood, who'd have run the lot of you Clankers out on a rail for half this racket. They're long gone now, and all that's left to keep the peace is one tired old sheriff and a lot of empty promises about settling down. I'm still waiting on that promise, MAXMANGO. I'm still waiting.

So hear me: lay down the fists. Let an old town rest a while. Every clang and every crack in this pavement is on you rowdy contraptions, and I am plum out of patience for it.

— Sheriff Cole Ironside
