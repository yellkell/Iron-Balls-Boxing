# The Gasket Gazette — No. 132

**WEDNESDAY, NOVEMBER 4** · _RELIEF_

## A DAY OF PERFECT SILENCE: NOT ONE FIST RAISED IN GASKET

*Sheriff Ironside reports, with a catch in his throat, that the whole town heard nothing at all.*

Friends, I have waited a long time to write this sentence: NOBODY FOUGHT. Not one iron ball was thrown. Not one fist met one jaw. The street was so quiet I could hear the wind whistle through the empty saloons, and I swear I heard a tumbleweed apologise for the noise it made.

I sat on my porch from sunup to sundown and not a single Clanker came rattling by to ruin it. No flames. No clanging. No swaggering. Just the sweet hush Gasket had when it was a proper town, back when real flesh-and-blood folk walked these planks. Fifty years they've been gone, and today, for a few precious hours, it felt like they might wander home. I won't lie to you, my eyes got a little damp. Rust, most likely. Rain gets in everywhere.

To the rabble, I say this: look what you can do when you simply sit still! It costs you nothing. Keep it up. I'll even fetch the lemonade, once I've had my joints seen to and a quart of oil down the hatch. A man my age needs his oil, as any man of honest flesh does.

One quiet day does not fix a town. But it's a start, and tonight I sleep without cotton in my ears. Don't you dare ruin it tomorrow.

— Sheriff Cole Ironside
