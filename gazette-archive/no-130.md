# The Gasket Gazette — No. 130

**MONDAY, NOVEMBER 2** · _RELIEF_

## NOT A SINGLE FIST FLEW IN GASKET

*Sheriff weeps happy oil as the town sleeps through a whole Monday morning.*

Friends, I have waited a long time to write this: nothing happened. Not one iron ball was thrown. Not one fuse was lit. Not one Clanker came rattling down Main Street to turn my peaceful town into a shooting gallery. The silence was so fine I could hear the wind whistle through the old saloon.

I sat on the porch all night and listened to the quiet. It was the sweetest sound I've known since the last honest, flesh-and-blood neighbours packed their wagons and left this place fifty years ago. They'd have loved a night like this. I like to think they'd have stayed.

Now, don't get me wrong. I know a few of you rowdy contraptions will be back by supper, swinging at anything that moves. But for one blessed day, you let an old lawman rest. I'm so moved my joints are squeaking. Must be the damp. Or this wasting sickness, turning my good flesh to cold rivets. Either way, I need a lie-down and a quart of oil. Keep it quiet, Clankers. I'm begging you.

— Sheriff Cole Ironside
