# The Gasket Gazette — No. 95

**MONDAY, SEPTEMBER 28** · _UNEASE_

## GASKET DRAWS ONE QUIET BREATH — THEN A NEW SHADOW CROSSES THE DEPOT

*Not a single fist flew today, but the newest rivet in town has already earned the Sheriff's full and suspicious attention.*

Mark it in the ledger, friends, and mark it twice: not one iron ball crossed this town today. No fireballs. No fists. No fool posse marching past the town line to go slug something the size of a barn. I sat behind this desk from sunup to sundown with nothing to report but the wind working loose a shutter down on Main, and for a few good hours I let myself believe Gasket had finally learned some manners.

Then the depot bell rang anyway. A fresh set of gears rolled in under the name IRON-7EDD, dust still on its boots, eyes wide as a barn door at everything it passed. Hasn't thrown a punch. Hasn't so much as raised a fist that I've heard tell of. But I've swept enough scorch marks off this street to know that look, and I know exactly how long the quiet lasts once a new face starts admiring the saloon.

I'll confess my own joints don't care for the excitement either way — creak something awful when the weather turns, same as they always have, never mind what any fool doctor might read into that. A decent flesh-and-blood lawman keeps his aches to himself. It's been fifty years since the honest folk who built this town packed up their wagons and rode east, leaving the rest of us to rattle around in what's left of it, and I like to think they'd have known how to greet a stranger without a single window getting broken.

So here's my one demand of you, IRON-7EDD, same as I've given every wide-eyed arrival before you: unpack quiet, keep your fists to yourself, and let an old town rest. Gasket bought itself one whole day of peace. I intend to defend it.

— Sheriff Cole Ironside
