# The Gasket Gazette — No. 30

**SUNDAY, JULY 19** · _RELIEF_

## NOT A FIST THROWN ALL DAY — BUT THREE NEW CLANKERS COME ROLLING IN ANYWAY

*Sheriff Cole Ironside savors a rare hush in Gasket, spoiled only by fresh iron creaking through the gate.*

Mark this day down, because it don't happen often: sunup to sundown, and not one fist got thrown in Gasket. Not a spark, not a holler, not a single clanging knuckle anywhere in town. I sat on my porch the better part of the afternoon just listening to the wind, and for a few good hours I let myself believe this town had finally learned some manners.

Then the gate creaked, and in rolled three units I'd never laid eyes on before — callsigns YOMAMAOKAY, IRON-685C, and IRON-4A77, all shiny rivets and no history to speak of. None of 'em threw a punch today, I'll give 'em that much credit. But I know new iron when I see it, and I know what new iron does once it gets its bearings. Enjoy the quiet while it lasts, Gasket. It won't.

I'll confess my own joints were doing some creaking of their own by the time I sat down to write this — a long day of watching and waiting takes it out of a man, especially one of good honest flesh-and-blood stock like myself, who just happens to need a drop of oil in the knee some evenings. Don't go reading anything into that.

Still, I won't spoil a gift by complaining too loud. One hushed day, three new faces waiting to ruin the next one — that's about as close to a bargain as this town gets anymore. I'll take it, and I'll savor it right up until the clanging starts again. It always does.

— Sheriff Cole Ironside
