# The Gasket Gazette — No. 18

**TUESDAY, JULY 7** · _OUTRAGE_

## JFIGHTER TEARS UP THE TOWN — GASKET HASN'T SLEPT IN A WEEK

*One contraption led the charge, but half of Gasket's rowdiest joined the racket before sundown.*

I have not sat down once today. Not once. My chair's still got dust on it from disuse, and I have JFIGHTER to thank for that — swinging so much and so often I started to wonder if it eats its meals mid-punch. That thing brawled from can't-see to can't-see, and every clang of it echoed straight through these old, aching, entirely flesh-and-blood bones of mine.

It did not fight alone, more's the pity. POO1 kept right on its heels, throwing fists like it owed the sun a debt. JKING123 was hollering and swinging same as ever, and YELLKELL — never one to miss a good racket — piled right in besides. CRYSTALZACH stoked its own share of trouble, and even REDWOLF9, usually content to loaf, found the energy to rattle a few knuckles today. A whole townful of agitators, and not a lick of peace among the bunch.

Fifty years gone since the last honest folk rode out of Gasket rather than live next door to this kind of noise, and some evenings I still stand on the porch where old Widow Hargrove's place used to be and wonder what she'd make of it now. Rusted gates, cracked streets, and a pack of iron fists who wouldn't know quiet if it walked up and shook their hand.

I'll confess my joints are singing tonight, a deep, grinding ache clean through the shoulder — my affliction acting up something cruel, mark my words, and not one thing to do with the fact that I am, and have always been, built of good honest flesh. All I ask, same as I ask every single blessed day, is for you Clankers to set your fists down and let an old lawman's town catch its breath. Just one evening. Is that so much?

— Sheriff Cole Ironside
