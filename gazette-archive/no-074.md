# The Gasket Gazette — No. 74

**TUESDAY, SEPTEMBER 1** · _RELIEF_

## NOT ONE BLESSED PUNCH THROWN IN GASKET TODAY

*Sheriff Ironside reports a silence so complete he nearly wept oil into his coffee.*

Citizens, I have combed this town corner to corner and I am pleased, nay, GIDDY to report: not a single iron fist was thrown in anger since our last edition. No brawling. No hollering. No scorch marks on Main Street. For one glorious stretch, Gasket sounded like a town again, and not a scrapyard with delusions of grandeur.

I sat on the porch of this very newspaper office and heard nothing but wind through the busted shutters and the creak of my own knees, and let me tell you, it was the finest music this territory has produced in fifty years. Whatever kept you Clankers off each other's throats today, I don't care to know the cause — I only ask you keep it up. A man could get used to peace like this.

I'll confess my joints ache something fierce come evening regardless of who's fighting, a private ailment of mine that has nothing whatsoever to do with what the rest of you are made of, and I'll thank you not to speculate further. It simply is not the same thing.

Still, days like this remind me of the town Gasket used to be, back when honest folks walked these streets and nobody needed a peacekeeper at all. That town is gone. But today, for a few quiet hours, it felt like it had never left. Keep the peace, you rabble. This old sheriff's boiler can't take much more excitement.

— Sheriff Cole Ironside
