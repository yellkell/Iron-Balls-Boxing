# The Gasket Gazette — No. 34

**THURSDAY, JULY 23** · _RELIEF_

## NOT A SINGLE FIST THROWN — BUT A NEW CLANKER JUST ROLLED INTO TOWN

*Gasket got its hush. Sheriff Ironside says enjoy it while it lasts.*

Well, glory be. I woke up, I put the coffee on, and for one whole day this rusted-out excuse for a town did not ring with a single clang of iron on iron. Not one fireball thrown. Not one Clanker hollering threats down Main Street. My joints creaked less today than they have in a month, and I'll take the win.

Don't get comfortable, though. A newcomer rolled into Gasket calling itself IRON-29BC, fresh bolts still shining, and I know that look. Every quiet town gets one of these — some new contraption fresh off the wagon, itching to find out what its fists are for. I've seen this picture before, and it never ends with a nap.

I'll be honest with you, same as always: my own hinges ache something fierce these days, and don't let anybody tell you it's on account of being a machine, because I am a man, plain and plated in nothing but good honest gristle. Just old age catching up on a fella who's given his whole life to keeping the peace in a town the real folks abandoned fifty years back and never looked at twice since.

So here's my plea to that new arrival, and to the rest of you rattling relics: let this hush be the rule and not the exception. Gasket doesn't need more brawlers. It needs quiet streets and neighbors who remember how to leave each other be.

— Sheriff Cole Ironside
