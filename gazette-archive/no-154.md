# The Gasket Gazette — No. 154

**THURSDAY, NOVEMBER 26** · _THANKFUL_

## Seventeen Days of Peace: Sheriff Gives Thanks to an Empty Table

*Thanksgiving dawns on a Gasket with no bouts, no brawls and no war parties, and the law carves a turkey he cannot explain.*

Seventeen days. Happy Thanksgiving, Gasket Territory, and what a Thanksgiving it is. Not one Clanker squared up on Main Street since our last edition. Not one flaming iron ball went whistling over the feed store. Not one war party marched off to the wastes to go slugging mountains. The whole town is so quiet I can hear the tumbleweeds thinking. I have waited fifty years for a holiday this peaceful, and I intend to enjoy every silent minute of it.

I sat down at the long table at noon sharp, forty places set, thirty-nine of them empty, same as every year since the good flesh-and-blood families packed their wagons and left. I said grace for each of them by name. Then I carved the turkey, which I found in a crate behind the old general store. It was a fine bird, I will grant you, though it clanked when I set it down and the carving knife threw sparks. I ate a whole drumstick before I noticed it had a bolt in the middle. My affliction must be spreading to my taste buds now. That cruel sickness takes everything from a man, one honest organ at a time.

Now, a word of thanks, and I do not hand these out lightly. XYFUME, JAKETHEPRO, YOMAMAOKAY, JFIGHTER2, and every other rattling contraption hiding in its shed today: you kept your fists down. You let an old town rest on its one good holiday. I would sooner gargle nails than call any of you a neighbour, but I will say this much. Seventeen days of hush is the finest gift a Clanker ever gave this place, and it is the only gift I ever wanted.

So tonight I will sit on my porch with a full belly and a squeaky hip, watching the sun go down over a street with no fighting in it. Keep it that way. Tomorrow is just another Friday, and I would very much like it to stay that dull.

— Sheriff Cole Ironside
