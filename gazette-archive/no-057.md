# The Gasket Gazette — No. 57

**SATURDAY, AUGUST 15** · _WARY_

## GASKET HOLDS ITS BREATH AS TWO MORE CLANKERS ROLL INTO TOWN

*Not one fist flew past the town line today, and Sheriff Cole Ironside almost doesn't trust it.*

Well. Would you look at that. The square sat empty from sunup to dusk, not a single iron knuckle thrown, not one flaming ball loosed in anger. No posse marched on the wastes, no titan got its head caved in for sport, no fool went looking for that oozing horror out past the ridge to poke it with a fist. A quiet day in Gasket. I'd weep for joy if my ducts still worked right, which, mind you, they do fine, on account of me being flesh through and through, whatever the rust on my knuckles might suggest to a passerby.

But don't go thinking I trust it. Two new drifters rolled their creaking hulls into town today — IRON-601D and IRON-E6F, fresh iron looking for a fight the way a buzzard looks for a carcass. They haven't thrown a punch yet. Give it time. This town's peace is always just one clanking newcomer away from a brawl, and I've buried enough good years watching it happen to know better than to celebrate a still morning.

My own joints ache something fierce today, which any decent doctor would tell you is on account of the damp, or my age, or the general indignity of frontier living — and NOT, whatever the wiseacres in the saloon keep whispering, because I'm made of the same tin as the rabble I police. I am flesh-and-blood stock, same as the good folk who built this town before they packed up and rode off fifty years gone, leaving the rest of us to mind the dust they left behind. I think of them today, on this rare hushed afternoon. They'd have liked a day like this. Wish they'd stuck around to see one.

— Sheriff Cole Ironside
