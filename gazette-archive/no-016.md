# The Gasket Gazette — No. 16

**SUNDAY, JULY 5** · _OUTRAGE_

## GASKET RATTLES AS THE SAME IRON FISTS WON'T STOP SWINGING

*Sheriff Cole Ironside pleads for one quiet hour and gets two hundred more brawls instead.*

Two hundred brawls. Two HUNDRED. I did not misprint that, and Lord knows I wish I had. Gasket used to be a town where a man could hear his own thoughts over the wind. Now all I hear is knuckles on iron and iron on iron, morning to midnight, courtesy of the same rowdy names filling my ledger week after week.

JKING123 is the worst of the lot this stretch — swinging so often I half expect the contraption sleeps standing up, fists still cocked. REDWOLF9 ain't far behind, brawling like the sun owes it money. YELLKELL keeps at it too, and CRYSTALZACH and POO1 are matching each other blow for blow like it's a competition to see who tires me out first. It is not a competition. I am already tired.

And now two fresh faces, IRON-4C25 and IRON-7882, come rattling into my town looking for a fight before they've even learned where the saloon is. Wonderful. More racket. More rust in the street. Fifty years since the last flesh-and-blood soul packed a wagon and rode clean out of Gasket rather than put up with this, and I don't blame them one bit some mornings — though don't you go telling anyone a lawman said so.

My knees ache something fierce tonight, creaking like a rusted gate, and I'll thank you not to ask why. A man of my upstanding, flesh-and-blood constitution simply gets weary, is all. What I want — what I have always wanted — is for these Clankers to lay down their fists and let an old town rest. Just once. Is that so much to ask of a pack of tin-fisted brawlers who don't know the meaning of quiet?

— Sheriff Cole Ironside
