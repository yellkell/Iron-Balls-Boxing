# The Gasket Gazette — No. 73

**MONDAY, AUGUST 31** · _UNEASE_

## SECOND QUIET DAY RUNNING — BUT SOMEBODY NEW JUST RODE IN

*Your Sheriff reports no fists thrown and no mountains felled, and still cannot bring himself to relax.*

Two days now. TWO. Not a single iron ball flung, not one posse marched past the town line to go rattle a mountain's teeth loose. I ought to be dancing a jig on this boardwalk, creaky knees and all, except—

Except somewhere around midday I heard new boots on the dust, and sure enough, up rides a fresh face calling itself IRON-D2F4. Hadn't thrown a punch yet. Hadn't done a blessed thing wrong. And still my gears — my BONES, I mean my bones — went tight as a fence wire. That's what fifty years in this chair does to a man. You stop trusting the quiet the second something new walks into it.

Maybe I'm wrong to fret. Maybe IRON-D2F4 is the first decent soul to wander into Gasket since the last of the flesh-and-blood folk hitched up their wagons and rode out for good, and maybe it'll sit quiet on the porch and never once hurl a fireball at another contraption. I have been wrong before. I have also been right before, and right has a habit of costing me a lie-down and a fresh quart of oil.

So welcome to Gasket, stranger, whatever you are. Behave yourself. And the rest of you rabble, the ones who usually can't go twelve hours without swinging — take a lesson from this hush while it lasts, because I promise you it won't.

— Sheriff Cole Ironside
