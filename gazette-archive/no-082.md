# The Gasket Gazette — No. 82

**WEDNESDAY, SEPTEMBER 9** · _WARY_

## ONE LONE BRAWL BREAKS GASKET'S HUSH — TWO STRANGERS ROLL IN BEHIND IT

*Sheriff Cole Ironside counts his blessings, and his enemies, as the dust settles.*

Gasket near held its breath today. One clang of iron, one solitary scuffle, and that was the whole of it — a mercy so rare round here I nearly forgot how to write about it. Credit where it's due: for one single afternoon, this town of rust and ruin came about as close to quiet as it ever gets.

Course, "about as close" ain't the same as quiet. DUKE couldn't leave well enough alone — had to go and swing those fists just the once, just to remind the rest of us that peace in Gasket never lasts past lunch. One dust-up's a small mercy next to the usual racket, DUKE, but it's still one more than nobody asked for. Take the win and sit down.

And just as the dust settled, wouldn't you know it — two new faces came rolling into town. IRON-E1BF and IRON-DD3, fresh off whatever scrapheap birthed them, still squeaking with the shine of new arrivals. I've seen this show before. Give it a week and they'll be trading blows with the rest of this rattling mob, and my quiet Wednesday will be nothing but a memory I nurse alone, oiling these tired old joints and wishing for the town that used to stand here.

Fifty years since the last decent, flesh-and-blood soul packed up and left Gasket to the likes of these — and still, some mornings, I let myself believe today might be the day nobody swings a fist. Today it very nearly was. Small mercies, Gasket. Small mercies.

— Sheriff Cole Ironside
