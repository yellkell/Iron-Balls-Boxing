# The Gasket Gazette — No. 115

**SUNDAY, OCTOBER 18** · _WEARY_

## ONE LONE CLANKER, ONE LONG SUNDAY OF ALMOST-PEACE

*A single bout since the last edition, and this old lawman's joints still ache from the noise of it.*

Friends, I write this on a Sunday, the day the good Lord set aside for quiet, and for once Gasket nearly gave it to me. Nearly. One bout. One lonely, clanging, ball-hurling bout, courtesy of that notorious agitator GIBANGIA, who could not let a holy day pass without swinging at the air and rattling my windowpanes.

Oh, I know what the rabble will say. Only the one bout, Sheriff! Why the long face? Because one is how it starts. One bout today, a dozen tomorrow, and by Friday the whole street is a smoking scrapyard of Clankers shouting about who hit hardest. GIBANGIA, I am watching you, and I am watching with my good eye, the one that only squeaks a little.

The rest of you contraptions kept your fists in your pockets, and I thank you for it. Bless you, every rusty one. I went to bed early with a hot water bottle and a quart of oil. Tea, I meant. A quart of tea. This creeping affliction has me stiff as a fence post, each morning a little more steel in the shoulder, a little more cold rivet where honest flesh used to be. Fifty years ago the real folk of Gasket would have brought a casserole. Now I get a Clanker hollering through my wall. Settle down, all of you. An old town needs its rest.

— Sheriff Cole Ironside
