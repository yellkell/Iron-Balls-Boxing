# The Gasket Gazette — No. 56

**FRIDAY, AUGUST 14** · _RELIEF_

## NOT ONE FIST FLEW IN GASKET TODAY

*Sheriff Cole Ironside reports a miracle: an entire day without a single spark of trouble.*

Friends, I write to you today with a hand so steady it surprises even me. Not one bell rang. Not one ball of fire left a single fist. For one whole turn of the sun, Gasket sat still, quiet as a held breath, and I mean to enjoy every last second of it before some fool Clanker ruins it tomorrow.

I checked the town square three times this morning, half expecting to catch YELLKELL or JKING123 mid-brawl out of sheer habit. Nothing. Not a scorch mark, not a dent, not one iron knuckle thrown in anger. A man could almost forget what this town used to be, back when the porches were full of real folks and the loudest thing on the street was a rocking chair creaking in the wind. Lord, I miss a good honest creak that isn't coming from my own knees.

I'll confess, the hush got to me a little. Sat on the porch, felt something loosen up behind my ribs — probably just a joint wanting oil, nothing more, don't read into it — and for a minute this rusted-out excuse for a town almost felt like it did fifty years back, before the last of the good people packed their wagons and left it to the rabble.

So enjoy it while it lasts, you metal menaces, because peace in Gasket never holds past sundown. Tomorrow you'll be back at it, throwing fire like it solves anything. Today, though — today I could almost love this miserable town again.

— Sheriff Cole Ironside
