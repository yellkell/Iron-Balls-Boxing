# The Gasket Gazette — No. 64

**SATURDAY, AUGUST 22** · _RELIEF_

## GASKET SLEEPS AT LAST — NOT ONE FIST THROWN

*Your Sheriff, for one blessed sunrise, has nothing to report — and he has never been happier to say so.*

Friends, neighbours, whatever's left of you — I write this edition with a steady hand and a lighter boiler, because for one whole stretch of daylight, not a single soul in this territory saw fit to throw a punch. No fireballs. No hollering. No Clanker dragging another Clanker through Main Street by the ankle. Just the wind, the creak of a loose shutter, and blessed, holy quiet.

I confess I don't rightly know what to do with myself. Fifty years I've kept this paper going mostly to catalogue the racket — and today the racket simply didn't show up. I sat on the porch of the Marshal's office a good long while just listening to nothing, and for a moment I could almost picture Gasket the way it was before the last of the flesh-and-blood folk packed their wagons and rode off, back when a quiet street meant peace and not just everybody sleeping off a brawl.

Don't mistake this for softness. Come tomorrow the iron fists will start swinging again same as always, and I'll be back here railing at every one of you rust-buckets by name. But today — today I'm allowing myself a rare, undignified grin, creaky joints and all. A man's plating can only take so much excitement, and I'll happily trade the drama for one more evening of peace and a long, well-earned sit-down.

So mark it in the record, whoever's still reading: Edition sixty-four, and Sheriff Cole Ironside has nothing but good news. Enjoy it. It won't last.

— Sheriff Cole Ironside
