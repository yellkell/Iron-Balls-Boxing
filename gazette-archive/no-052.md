# The Gasket Gazette — No. 52

**MONDAY, AUGUST 10** · _RELIEF_

## NOT ONE PUNCH THROWN — GASKET FINALLY GETS ITS QUIET

*Not a single iron fist swung since sunup, and this old lawman intends to enjoy every blessed second of it.*

Friends, I hardly know how to say this without my voice catching, so I will just say it plain: not one single Clanker in this town threw a punch today. Not one flaming ball flew. Not one poor storefront took a fist through its window. I have walked this street since sunup and heard nothing but wind, and I tell you, it is the sweetest sound Gasket has made in longer than I care to count.

There is a new face rolled into town — calls itself IRON-F06B, all rivets and swagger, the way they always arrive. I have watched it close all day. Not one blow thrown yet. See that it stays that way, newcomer. This town has had enough of its peace ground into scrap.

I will confess my joints ache something fierce on a quiet day like this — a strange thing for flesh and blood to say, I know, and I will not be dwelling on it further. The old folks who built this town, back before the last of the honest kind packed up their wagons and rode clean out fifty years gone, would have wept to see one hushed evening like this. I like to think, somewhere, they are smiling at it.

So let it be known, in ink, for the record: Gasket had itself a day of peace. Let it be the first of many, and let every rusted lout reading this take the hint before I am forced to write about you instead.

— Sheriff Cole Ironside
