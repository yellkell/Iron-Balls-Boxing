# The Gasket Gazette — No. 43

**SATURDAY, AUGUST 1** · _OUTRAGE_

## FOUR STRANGERS ROLL INTO TOWN, GASKET RINGS LIKE A SCRAPYARD ALL DAY

*Sheriff Cole Ironside surveys a Saturday overrun with newcomers and one Clanker who simply will not quit swinging.*

Four new faces rattled into Gasket since I last picked up my pen — IRON-00CF, BABYSINE, IRON-F8BF, and IRON-F053, wandering in through the dust like they own the place. Barely got their boots unpacked before they were elbow to elbow with the rest of the rabble, adding their racket to a town that was doing just fine without it.

And leading that racket, same as it ever is, was REDWOLFMETA — swinging fists from sunup clean past supper, in and out of the ring so many times I plain lost count. Won't stop. Can't stop. I don't believe the notion of a quiet Saturday has ever once crossed that thing's processor.

Makes my joints ache just watching it, and I mean that plain — my knees have been grinding since Tuesday, and don't let anybody tell you that's ordinary for a man of my modest years. Fifty years since the last real folks packed their wagons and rode out of Gasket for good, and this is what they left behind: a dusty square full of iron knuckleheads who think a punch-up passes for entertainment.

I built this paper to report news, not count knuckles, but there's precious little else stirring in this town besides the clang of somebody's fist finding somebody's face. Lay it down, the lot of you. Let an old lawman have one quiet Saturday before he rusts through entirely.

— Sheriff Cole Ironside
