# The Gasket Gazette — No. 40

**WEDNESDAY, JULY 29** · _DISGUST_

## GASKET DOZES OFF — EXCEPT FOR ONE STUBBORN RATTLER

*One name kept Sheriff Cole Ironside from a full and holy hush over Gasket Territory.*

Praise be, this town near slept through the whole day. Near. I said near, and I meant it, because one contraption out there just couldn't let a fella rest. YELLKELL, that's the one, up and at it not once but twice while every other rusted soul in this territory had the good sense to sit still and let an old lawman's nerves settle.

Twice, folks. In a town this size, that's a racket you can hear from the jailhouse porch. I sat here with my joints aching something fierce — don't ask, it's the damp, always is — hoping for one whole day where nobody hurled a fireball at their neighbor for sport. Instead I got YELLKELL, rattling around like it owns the street, clanking iron into iron while the rest of Gasket had the decency to keep its fists to itself.

I remember when this town didn't need decency reminders. Fifty years now since the last flesh-and-blood folks packed their wagons and rode clean out of Gasket, leaving it to the likes of these metal troublemakers. They'd never have stood for one lone brawler keeping the whole street on edge. I stood for it today, and it cost me a headache I'll be feeling in my back plate — my BACK, mind you, not some gear box, whatever the doc claims.

So hear me, YELLKELL: a quiet town is a gift, not a dare. Rest those fists. Let Gasket have its nap. The rest of you kept the peace today — don't let one restless clanker talk you out of doing it again tomorrow.

— Sheriff Cole Ironside
