## 3. The full pitch (landing page / press blurb)

### FIRE FIGHT
**Bare-knuckle boxing, at a distance, with fire.**

You don't punch your opponent — you punch fire *at* them. Two flaming iron
balls orbit your fists. Hold the trigger and a ball spins up around your hand;
whip a real punch and let go to hurl it across the gap; pull the trigger again
to call it blazing back to your palm — and a recalled ball that passes straight
through your rival on the way home still counts. Your orbiting ball is your
shield too: it parries incoming fire out of the air. Dodge with your whole
body, but don't lean off your platform — the arena will burn you for it.

It looks like an industrial future fight club: gunmetal plate, hazard-amber
striping, shoulder-heavy mech avatars and chunky mechanical gauntlets, all
rendered in passthrough so the fight happens in *your* room, with anvil-clang
metal underneath it. Every ball drags a thick comet trail. An invisible cage
keeps stray fire off your walls.

And it's built to compete:

- **1v1** — best-of-five duels, spatial voice chat so your rival's trash talk
  pans with their head.
- **2v2** — team up and coordinate fire.
- **Free-for-all** — last one standing.

Plus aim training and a sparring bot to warm up on.

**No app store. No download. No install.** It runs in your headset's web
browser.

> **Play now:** in your headset browser, go to **yellkell.com/ff** and press **Enter**.
